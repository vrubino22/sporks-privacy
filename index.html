Privacy Policy

App: Sporks
Developer: Vince Rubino
Effective date: May 22, 2026

Data We Collect

Sporks has no backend servers and does not collect or store your personal data itself. Online multiplayer uses Apple's Game Center infrastructure — see the section below for details.

Local Multiplayer (Nearby Devices)

The local multiplayer feature uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to connect nearby devices over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth network. The only information shared between devices is your device's display name (used as your player name) and game state messages. This data never leaves your local network and is never sent to us or any third party.

Online Multiplayer (Game Center)

Online multiplayer requires signing in to Game Center with your Apple ID. When you play online:

  • Your Game Center display name is visible to opponents during a match.
  • Apple assigns each player a scoped, per-game identifier used to route match data — this is not your Apple ID or email address.
  • Game state messages (cards, moves, scores) are transmitted through Apple's Game Center servers over the internet.

Sporks does not store any of this data on its own servers. Apple's Privacy Policy governs how Game Center handles your information: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Analytics & Advertising

Sporks contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Sporks does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
