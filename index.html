Privacy Policy

App: Sporks
Developer: Vince Rubino
Effective date: May 21, 2026

Data We Collect

Sporks does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. No accounts, logins, or profiles are required.

Local Multiplayer

The multiplayer feature uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to connect nearby devices over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth network. The only information shared between devices is your device's display name (used as your player name) and game state messages. This data never leaves your local network and is never sent to us or any third party.

Analytics & Advertising

Sporks contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Sporks does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
